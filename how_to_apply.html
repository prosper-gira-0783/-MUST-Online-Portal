How to Apply

Follow these steps to prepare and submit your application to MUST.

1

Create an Account

Choose Create Account on the portal and enter your full name, email address, phone number and password. Use an email address that you can access.

2

Sign In

After creating your account, return to the sign-in page and log in using your registered email address and password.

3

Complete the Application Form

Provide the requested personal details, academic background and programme choices. Enter information accurately and make sure names and dates match your documents.

4

Prepare Required Documents

Have the academic certificates/results and any other documents required for your application category ready. Follow the portal's instructions for file formats and uploads.

5

Review and Submit

Review every section before submitting. Follow the official instructions for application fees, payment evidence and submission confirmation where applicable.

Important Information

Entry requirements, application deadlines, fees and required documents may change. Always confirm the current details using MUST's official application guidelines.
